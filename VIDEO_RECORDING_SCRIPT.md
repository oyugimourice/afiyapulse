## Recording Tips

### Before Recording
1. **Practice the script 3-4 times** to get timing right
2. **Prepare all screens** in advance (open tabs, login ready)
3. **Test audio levels** - speak clearly and at moderate pace
4. **Close unnecessary applications** to avoid notifications
5. **Use a timer** to track 3-minute limit

### During Recording
1. **Speak clearly and confidently** - no "ums" or "ahs"
2. **Maintain steady pace** - not too fast, not too slow
3. **Show, don't tell** - let the application speak for itself
4. **Keep cursor movements smooth** - no erratic clicking
5. **Stay within time limit** - practice makes perfect

### After Recording
1. **Review the video** - check audio and video quality
2. **Verify timing** - must be under 3 minutes
3. **Check for errors** - no typos visible, no crashes
4. **Add captions** if possible (accessibility)
5. **Export in high quality** - 1080p MP4

## Backup Plans

### If Something Goes Wrong
1. **Application crashes**: Have a backup recording ready
2. **Audio issues**: Re-record just the audio and overlay
3. **Timing too long**: Cut less important sections
4. **Timing too short**: Add more detail to narration

### Alternative Approaches
1. **Screen recording + voiceover**: Record screen first, add voice later
2. **Multiple takes**: Record in sections, edit together
3. **Slides + demo**: Mix slides with live demo
4. **Animated walkthrough**: Use screen captures with annotations
